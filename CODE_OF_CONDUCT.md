## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the **VEYA SDK** community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

### Positive behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Acknowledging the expertise of cryptography, systems, and EVM contributors alike
- Reporting security issues through private channels ([SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) rather than public exploit discussion

### Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (keys, `~/.veya` paths, internal emails) without explicit permission
- Sharing exploit details or live key material in issues or pull requests
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, including GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests, documentation (`docs/`, README, examples), official channels including security@veyanet.tech, and events representing VEYA.

---

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to **conduct@veyanet.tech**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Community leaders will respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

| Impact | Consequence |
|--------|-------------|
| **Correction** | Private written warning; clarity around violation |
| **Warning** | Warning with consequences for continued behavior |
| **Temporary ban** | Temporary exclusion from community interaction |
| **Permanent ban** | Permanent exclusion for sustained or severe violations |

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
